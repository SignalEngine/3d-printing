# Flexi Leash Poop Bag Holder with Treat-Pouch Loop and Bone Holes

Remix of **Heavy-Duty Flexi Leash Poop Bag Holder** by [CREATOR NAME] — https://makerworld.com/en/models/2003956
All credit for the original holder design goes to them. This remix adds a hanging loop and a dog-bone look.

## What's new in this remix
- **Treat-pouch loop** under the lid. Clip on a treat bag, keys, or a torch. The bar is 3.6 mm thick, so it fits standard snap hooks and swivel clips with a gate opening of 4.5 mm or more. The hole is a 6.3 × 5.2 mm teardrop shape, and the loop has a 45° underside, so it prints without supports.
- **Dog-bone cut-outs:** 18 bone-shaped holes replace the original hexagon pattern.
- **Sits flat on the bed:** the model is re-aligned upright, so the whole threaded end touches the plate for better adhesion.
- The **POOP letters stay separate parts**, so you can print them in a second colour with AMS.

## Print settings (tested on a Bambu Lab A1)
- Material: PLA (PETG if it lives on the leash in hot weather)
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Brim: on (the threaded end is a thin ring)
- Supports: [CONFIRM AFTER PRINT]
- Filament: about 23 cm³ (~29 g PLA)
- Print time: about 4 h at 0.20 mm Standard
- Size: 54.9 × 62.8 × 71.0 mm

## Fit
Same bag roll and leash fit as the original. If your snap hook's gate opens less than 4.5 mm, let me know in the comments.
